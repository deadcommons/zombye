# How Zombye is run

Zombye is open source and made in the open. This page says who decides what, and how a bigger change gets agreed.

## Who decides

- **Caretaker** looks after the project for Dead Commons and has the final say. As the project grows, the plan is for more of these decisions to move to the committee.
- **The committee** is the people who look after a part of the game (multiplayer, mods, art, sound, the website and so on). It advises on bigger changes and reviews the work in its area. To begin with it's Caretaker and whoever is invited from the regulars.
- **Contributors** are anyone who sends a change, reports a bug or helps in the Discord.

Dead Commons owns the Zombye name and logos. The code is MIT OR Apache-2.0 and our own art is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (see the licences). Nothing on this page changes that.

## Small changes

Bug fixes, small features, data tweaks and docs need no meeting:

1. Pick an open issue from the [project board](https://github.com/orgs/deadcommons/projects/2), or open one.
2. Say in the issue that you're taking it.
3. Send a small pull request, signed off (`git commit -s`, see CONTRIBUTING.md).
4. A maintainer reviews and merges it.

## Bigger changes: a proposal first

Write a proposal before you start on anything that changes how the game plays, its file formats, saves or network protocol, the modding API, a new dependency, or the licences or how the project is run.

1. Open a GitHub Discussion in the **Proposals** category. Say what you want to change, why, what it costs (performance, saves, mods) and what else you considered. A page is plenty.
2. Anyone can comment for at least a week.
3. The committee talks it over at the weekly call and says **yes**, **no**, or **not yet, because...**. Caretaker decides if the committee doesn't agree.
4. The decision and the reason go at the bottom of the discussion, so they can be found later.

## The weekly call

- Once a week, on the Discord, in the voice channel, for about 45 minutes. The time is pinned in #announcements.
- Anyone can listen in. Committee members and the author of each proposal can speak.
- The agenda is the proposals that have been open for a week, then anything blocked, then the plan for the next week. It's posted in Discord the day before.
- Short notes (decisions only) are posted in Discord and on each discussion afterwards.

## Joining the committee

Anyone who has contributed regularly for a couple of months and looks after a part of the game can be invited. Members are agreed at the weekly call. A member who has been away for three months can step back and come back whenever they like.

## Conduct

Be kind and assume good faith. Disagree about the work, not the person. Caretaker and the committee can remove anyone who makes the project worse for others.

## Changing this page

This page changes the way any bigger change does: a proposal, a week, the call.
