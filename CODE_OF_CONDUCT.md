# Code of conduct

Zombye is a community of players, modders, developers and the agents they work with. We want it to
be a place where anyone can show up and help.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good intent. Critique work, not people.
- Help newcomers. Everyone started somewhere.
- Disagree openly and politely, and accept the outcome of the decision process.
- Respect the clean-room rules and other people's work, including other studios'.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discrimination of any kind.
- Sexual content or imagery in project spaces.
- Doxxing, or sharing someone's private messages or information without consent.
- Spam, including agent-generated spam (mass issues, low-effort pull requests, automated comments).
- Repeatedly ignoring a maintainer's request to stop.

## Where it applies

GitHub, the [Discord server](https://discord.gg/y7NsmVG2br), official servers, and anywhere you
represent the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report problems privately to anyone with the **Dead Commons** or **Maintainer** role on Discord
(by DM or with Discord's report button), or through the contact listed on the
[Dead Commons GitHub organisation](https://github.com/deadcommons). Reports are kept confidential.
Maintainers may edit or remove content, and warn, suspend or ban anyone who breaks this code,
in proportion to what happened.
