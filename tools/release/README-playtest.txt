ZOMBYE: PLAYTEST BUILD
======================

Thanks for playing. This is an early build: things will break, and that is what we want to hear about.

STARTING
  Windows: run zombye.exe. If Windows SmartScreen says it protected your PC, click "More info",
           then "Run anyway" (the build isn't signed yet).
  Mac:     Apple Silicon only for now. Drag Zombye.app to Applications (or anywhere) and open it.
           It is signed and notarised by Apple, so it opens like any other app.
  Linux:   run ./zombye (x86_64; needs Vulkan drivers).

THE KEYS
  WASD move, Shift run, C sneak. Hold the right mouse button to aim; while aiming, left click
  attacks. Space shoves (or stamps on a zombie on the ground). E picks up or searches, Tab the
  inventory, H health, K skills, M the map, B sleep, F opens doors and windows. Esc is the menu,
  and Settings > Controls lists every key.
  The game starts full screen: Alt+Enter or F11 makes it a window and back, and it remembers.

SOMETHING WENT WRONG?
  Press F12 in the game: it saves a report (a screenshot, the log and your world) and opens its
  folder. Write a line about what happened in about.txt and send us the folder.
  If the game crashed, the next start tells you where the crash log is.

  Your saves, settings, logs and reports are in:
    Windows: %APPDATA%\Zombye
    Mac:     ~/Library/Application Support/Zombye
    Linux:   ~/.local/share/zombye

MULTIPLAYER
  Main menu > Multiplayer, then the address and password we gave you.
