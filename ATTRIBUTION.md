# Asset attribution

Every non-code asset in this repository (models, textures, animations, sounds, music, fonts,
images) has a row in the table below: what it is, who made it, how it was made, and its licence.
Add the row in the same commit as the file. Shaders (`assets/shaders/`) are source code written
by us and are licensed like the rest of the code, so they don't need a row.

**Policy (mandatory):** Zombye contains **no assets from other games** — no art, textures, models,
icons, UI graphics, sounds, music, fonts, text or map data from any other game or its mods, not
even as placeholders, and nothing traced, screenshot-cropped or recreated from them. Every asset
is one of:

- original work by Dead Commons or a Zombye contributor, published under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (see
  assets/LICENSE). Contributors grant their art and audio under CC BY 4.0 (see CONTRIBUTING.md);
- generated by us with an AI tool whose terms let us redistribute the output openly (record the
  tool and the plan it was made on — see below);
- public domain / CC0, OSI open source, or a Creative Commons licence that allows redistribution.

No third-party asset packs (Synty, marketplace packs and similar), even if you own them.
In the table, our own art and audio use the licence `CC BY-NC-SA 4.0`.
Until an asset exists, the game uses grey-box shapes.

**AI-generated assets:** fill in the *Generator* column with the tool and plan, for example
`Meshy (Pro)`. Generate on a paid plan where the free plan's output carries an attribution
licence (Meshy's free plan output is CC BY 4.0; paid plans give full ownership). Prompts and
reference images must be our own; never feed a generator screenshots or art from another game.
Steam requires AI-generated content to be disclosed, and this table is our record for that.
